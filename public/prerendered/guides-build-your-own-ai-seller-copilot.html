Build Your Own AI Seller Copilot

Your sales knowledge, trapped in your head, is worth nothing until an AI can use it. This guide shows you how to turn your playbooks — pricing rules, product research, listing optimization — into an AI copilot that answers like a specialist, not a generic chatbot. The full breakdown

Sellers are paying $49–$99/month for tools that show them dashboards.

But no dashboard ever answered the real question: "okay, but what should I DO next?"

That's the gap. And you don't need another subscription to close it — you can build your own AI seller copilot. No coding. Here's the exact path:

  1. Pick ONE niche and list the 10 questions your copilot must answer. (e.g. TikTok Shop affiliates: what to sell, what commission, who to partner with.)
  2. Pick your coding agent — the AI that writes the app for you. (For non-technical founders, start with Lovable: idea to live link, zero setup. The giants — Claude, ChatGPT, Google Antigravity — are brilliant coders, but they're built for developers.)
  3. Feed it your playbooks — plain-text files with your product research method, your pricing rules, your listing checklist.
  4. Connect the AI chat to those playbooks, so it answers like a specialist — not a generic chatbot.
  5. Test it with 5 real questions from your own business. Generic answer? Your playbook is thin. Fix the input, not the AI.
  6. Deploy it. You now have a live link — your own tool, built in an afternoon.

The complete guide covers which coding agent to pick (and which to avoid), the exact copy-paste prompt for every step, troubleshooting, and the advanced moves — live data, logins, turning it into a product you can sell.
