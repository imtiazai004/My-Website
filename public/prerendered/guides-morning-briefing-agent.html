The full breakdown

I build AI agents that brief you before your day starts.

Yesterday, Anthropic launched Claude Duet — a proactive AI agent for professionals and small-business owners. It doesn’t wait for you to ask questions. It watches your apps overnight, briefs you in the morning, and asks your approval before it touches anything sensitive.

They built a whole product around one pain I hear from every business owner I work with: you start the day blind. 40+ unread emails. WhatsApp messages from three time zones. Today’s appointments in one app, pending orders in another, unpaid invoices in a third. Your first hour — every single day — burned just figuring out what matters.

So instead of joining a waitlist for someone else’s generic agent, I build the Morning Briefing Agent — wired into YOUR business:

  1. The Collectors — Gmail, Google Calendar and your orders/invoices source pull everything that happened overnight.
  2. The Ranker — every item scored by one rule: money waiting first, customers waiting second, routine last — then drafted into one short brief.
  3. The Delivery — one WhatsApp message at 6:00 AM, readable in five minutes with your coffee.
  4. The Approval Lock — the agent NEVER sends, replies or pays on its own. It lists what needs your decision, drafts the action, and moves only when you reply YES.

The math: 45–60 minutes of morning triage a day is ~5 hours a week, ~260 hours a year — about $6,500 of owner time at $25/hour. The agent cuts it to a 5-minute read. And the lead who messaged at midnight? He’s in your 6 AM brief with a reply already drafted — answered before your competitors open their inbox.

The guide includes the full build roadmap: the exact ranking prompt, the no-code route (Make or n8n + the Claude or OpenAI API + WhatsApp Cloud API) and the code route (Node/Python + Gmail and Calendar APIs + a cron on Vercel or Railway), the approval-lock wiring, and the 7-day test that tunes the brief to your business. Download it above and wake up briefed.
