🎯 Core Product Analysis · 10 Features

Everything about a product — in seconds

Click one button on any product page and get a complete breakdown. No setup, no API key, no account needed.

Product Score (0–100)

Composite score based on price sweet spot, demand signals, competition level, and profit margin potential. Higher = better opportunity.

GO / CAUTION / NO-GO Verdict

A clear decision checking 6 criteria: price range, review count, demand proof, brand lock, margin viability, and category trend.

Auto-Inject Quick Bar

A live score bar appears automatically at the bottom of every product page — no clicks needed. Score, BSR, price, revenue, and margin instantly visible.

Listing Quality Score (A–F)

Grades how well a competitor optimized their listing. A low-grade competitor is your opening — beat them with a better title, bullets, and images.

BuyBox Detection

Detects if one seller dominates the Buy Now button. If locked, the score drops 25 points — a critical red flag before you commit to inventory.

Review Velocity

Estimated new reviews per month. High velocity means the product is growing fast — a signal to move quickly or reconsider the competition level.

Seller Count

How many sellers compete on this listing. Multiple sellers prove demand. One seller means BuyBox risk. Critical intel before entering any niche.

FBA Detection

Detects whether the top seller uses Amazon FBA. FBA sellers get the Prime badge and BuyBox preference — know the barrier before you invest.

Variation Finder

All color/size/pack variants shown as clickable ASIN chips. Find the under-competitive variant that other sellers haven't spotted yet.

Risk Analysis (4 Dimensions)

Every product rated across Demand Risk, Supply Risk, Regulatory Risk, and Margin Risk — each Low / Medium / High. Know exactly what you're walking into.

FAQ

Common questions

Is ProfitScout really free?

Yes — 100% free, forever. No subscription, no trial, no credit card. AI features require your own free Google Gemini API key.

Does it work on all Amazon marketplaces?

Yes. ProfitScout supports Amazon US, UK, CA, DE, FR, IT, ES, JP, and AU.

Do I need an API key to use it?

No. All 44 core features work with zero setup. The optional Gemini AI mode requires a free key from Google AI Studio.

Is my data safe?

All your leads, settings, and tracking data stay in your browser's local storage. Only anonymous product metadata (price, BSR) is synced for market intelligence.

Can I use it for sourcing research?

Yes — ProfitScout supports 6 sourcing platforms including Alibaba, AliExpress, 1688, and DHgate, with full sourcing score and price tier comparisons.
