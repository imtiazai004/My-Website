Dr. Tabassum Ajmal

Dr. Tabassum Ajmal is a Prosthodontist — a dental specialist trained in the restoration and replacement of teeth. Her expertise covers dental implants, veneers, crowns, whitening and complete smile designing.

She believes a dental visit should never be something you fear. That's why every treatment at Family Dental starts with listening — understanding your concerns, explaining your options in simple words, and proceeding only when you're fully comfortable.
