Preventive Dental Treatments

Regular preventive visits catch problems early, protect your enamel and save you from pain — and from bigger bills later. Here is everything we do to keep your smile healthy for life.

Book a Checkup ↗

Scaling / Polishing

Plaque and tartar slowly build up on your teeth, leading to gum disease, bad breath and tooth decay. Scaling carefully removes this hardened buildup from the tooth surface and from beneath the gumline, protecting your long-term oral health. Polishing follows — smoothing the enamel and lifting surface stains left by tea, coffee or smoking. The procedure is completely painless, and you walk out with a fresher, cleaner smile. Done every six months, it keeps gums healthy and problems away.

Root Canal Treatment

When deep decay or an injury infects the soft pulp inside your tooth, the result is severe pain and sensitivity. A root canal gently removes the infected pulp, disinfects the tiny root canals and seals them to prevent reinfection — saving your natural tooth and avoiding extraction. Thanks to modern rotary techniques and effective anesthesia, the treatment is relatively painless, much to most patients' surprise. Afterwards the tooth is restored with a filling or crown so it works and looks natural again.

Dental Fillings

Cavities form when mouth bacteria produce acids that erode your enamel. Left alone, decay spreads deeper — bringing pain and infection. A dental filling stops it: the decayed area is cleaned and sealed with composite resin, amalgam or glass ionomer, restoring the tooth's shape and strength. The procedure is quick and painless, and modern tooth-colored fillings blend invisibly with your natural teeth.

Temporary / Permanent Fillings

A temporary filling protects your tooth between visits — for example while a root canal is being completed or before a crown is placed — keeping you comfortable and the tooth sealed. A permanent filling is the long-term restoration: it seals the cavity completely, restores chewing function and blocks bacteria from re-entering. Depending on the cavity's location and your preference, we use composite, amalgam or ceramic materials.

Polishing / Removal of Stains

Daily habits — coffee, tea, soft drinks, smoking — leave stains that make teeth look dull and discolored. Polishing is a simple, painless procedure that buffs away these surface stains for a smooth, shiny finish. Usually done right after scaling, it also makes it harder for plaque to stick to your teeth. The result: a fresh, clean, brighter smile as part of your regular dental care.

Pit & Fissure Sealants

Your molars and premolars have deep grooves that trap food and bacteria — a favorite starting point for cavities, especially in children. Pit and fissure sealants are thin protective coatings painted directly into these grooves, sealing them shut against decay. The process takes minutes, is entirely painless, and gives lasting protection for kids and teenagers during their cavity-prone years.

Fluoride Varnish

Fluoride strengthens tooth enamel and makes it far more resistant to decay. Fluoride varnish is a highly concentrated gel painted onto the teeth, forming a protective layer that remineralizes early weak spots before they become cavities. It is especially valuable for children, cavity-prone adults and sensitive teeth. Application takes a couple of minutes, is completely painless, and protection lasts for months — particularly alongside good brushing and flossing.

Stabilization Splints (Night Guards)

Many people grind their teeth in their sleep without knowing it — a condition called bruxism. Over time it wears down enamel, causes jaw pain and headaches, and can even crack teeth or damage crowns and fillings. A stabilization splint (night guard) is a custom-made appliance worn over your teeth at night: it cushions grinding forces, protects your enamel and eases jaw tension. Better-protected teeth — and better sleep.
