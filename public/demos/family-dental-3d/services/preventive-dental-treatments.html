Preventive Dental Treatments

The best dental treatment is the one you never need. Our preventive treatments stop problems before they start — keeping your smile healthy for life.

Scaling / Polishing

Plaque and tartar build up on teeth over time, leading to gum disease, bad breath, and tooth decay. Scaling removes this hardened buildup from the surface of teeth and beneath the gums, preventing long-term damage. Polishing follows scaling and smooths out the enamel, removing surface stains caused by tea, coffee, or smoking. This painless procedure not only improves oral hygiene but also gives teeth a fresher, cleaner appearance. Regular scaling and polishing, done every six months, significantly reduces the risk of cavities and gum disease — the foundation of a healthy, long-lasting smile.

Root Canal Treatment

When a tooth becomes infected due to deep decay or an injury, the infection can spread to the nerves inside the tooth, causing severe pain and sensitivity. A root canal treatment involves cleaning out the infected pulp, disinfecting the root canals, and sealing them to prevent reinfection. This procedure saves the natural tooth, preventing the need for extraction. While many people fear root canals, modern techniques and anesthesia make them relatively painless. Once treated, the tooth is restored with a filling or crown so it looks and works naturally again.

Dental Fillings

Cavities develop when bacteria in the mouth produce acids that erode tooth enamel. If left untreated, decay can spread deeper, leading to pain and even infection. Dental fillings restore the damaged tooth by sealing the cavity with materials like composite resin, amalgam, or glass ionomer. This prevents further decay and strengthens the tooth's structure. The procedure is quick and painless, and modern tooth-colored fillings blend naturally with the surrounding teeth.

Temporary / Permanent Fillings

A temporary filling is used when a tooth requires further treatment, such as a root canal or crown placement. It protects the tooth and prevents discomfort until a permanent filling is placed. Permanent fillings are meant for long-term restoration, sealing the cavity completely and restoring the tooth's function while keeping bacteria out. Depending on the cavity's location and your preference, materials like composite, amalgam, or ceramic are used — the right filling for every situation.

Polishing / Removal of Stains

Everyday habits like drinking coffee, tea, or soft drinks cause stains on teeth, making them look dull and discolored. Polishing is a simple procedure that removes these surface stains, giving teeth a smooth and shiny finish. Often done after scaling, it also helps prevent plaque buildup by making it harder for bacteria to stick to the teeth. Completely painless, polishing keeps your smile fresh and clean as part of regular dental care.

Pit & Fissure Sealants

Molars and premolars have deep grooves that trap food and bacteria, leading to cavities — especially in children. Pit and fissure sealants are thin protective coatings applied into these grooves to block out decay. The process is quick and painless, making it an excellent preventive measure for children and teenagers. By sealing the grooves shut, sealants act as a barrier against food particles and bacteria, greatly reducing cavity risk in back teeth.

Fluoride Varnish

Fluoride strengthens tooth enamel and makes it more resistant to decay. Fluoride varnish is a highly concentrated gel applied to the teeth, forming a protective layer that helps remineralize early signs of decay. It is especially beneficial for children, people at high risk of cavities, and those with sensitive teeth. The application is quick and painless, and the benefits last for several months. Combined with proper brushing and flossing, regular fluoride treatment significantly reduces decay.

Stabilization Splints (Night Guards)

Many people unknowingly grind their teeth while sleeping — a condition called bruxism. This leads to tooth wear, jaw pain, headaches, and even fractures in severe cases. Stabilization splints, also known as night guards, are custom-made appliances worn over the teeth during sleep to prevent grinding damage. They protect the enamel, reduce jaw tension, and safeguard dental work like crowns or fillings. A night guard means better-protected teeth — and better sleep.
